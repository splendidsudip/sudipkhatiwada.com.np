Academic & Professional Track

I am a PhD Researcher in Civil Engineering (Structural Engineering specialization) at the University of Nevada, Las Vegas (UNLV)[cite: 1]. My research centers on Engineered Cementitious Composites (ECC), finite element structural modeling, and machine learning framework design for sustainable concrete mix optimizations[cite: 1].

My international career encompasses post-earthquake damage assessment and residential reconstruction design with the National Reconstruction Authority in Nepal[cite: 2]; developing novel anti-seismic protection devices (LiCoRD & SMarT-ROD) and bridge monitoring frameworks at the University of Cagliari, Italy[cite: 2]; and engineering design practice with WSP USA[cite: 2].
